# Visual overhaul and recoverable failure modes

This update keeps the game dependency-free and preserves version-two adventure saves. New fields have defaults when loading earlier saves.

- Overweight builds expose **Launch Anyway (Overweight)**. A fully equipped overloaded build may run an intentional failure trial even outside the normal budget/power checks; normal, non-overweight launch readiness still enforces those limits. The booster struggles, then a one-second explosion effect plays before the workshop recovery dialog. All component choices and personalization survive recovery. This is a fictional training scenario, not a physical claim that every excess kilogram causes a real explosion.
- Moon, Mars and Titan transit last **10, 15 and 30 active seconds**. Four hits remove the 100% hull in 25% steps, with 1.2 seconds of immunity per hit. Hull destruction offers Retry Transit or Abort to Main Menu. Retry retains equipment, discoveries and identity. Skipping never earns the Evasive Pilot bonus. Legacy transit timers are clamped to the active destination's duration.
- Debris includes carbonaceous/silicate rocks, foil satellite bodies with separated arrays, ribbed booster tanks, fairings and bolts. Motion and decorative spinning respect the existing reduced-motion setting where practical; gameplay obstacles still move.
- The workshop includes a 12-character alphanumeric callsign and three foil accents. Preferences are saved under `deepfrontier.personalization` and copied into each new expedition. Accent bands render on rocket, capsule, descent craft and rover trim. Foil names are artistic customization, not recalculated thermal properties. Retired White Ceramic saves safely fall back to Gold MLI.
- Completing and downlinking all three worlds unlocks the Grand Tour panorama, gold welcome ribbon and Chief Solar System Pioneer rank, independently of the normal score threshold. Existing completed logbook missions qualify.
- Rendering uses a 1200×700 virtual world with a uniform fit transform and letterboxing. Pointer coordinates invert the same transform. The scrolling workshop caps its canvas to the visible window so the parts list cannot stretch Pip off-screen. Touch controls remain docked to the screen rather than the virtual canvas; dialogs scroll within the viewport.
- Art additions include layered lunar crater rays and rock seams, Martian terraces and dust ripples, Titan curved wave crests and a faint artistic Saturn glow, blast deflectors and pad heat shimmer, engine sparks and expanded explosion debris. The approved eight-spoke wheel silhouette remains; hubs/treads now turn with distance travelled, including vertical motion, and suspension motion follows rotation.
- Synthesized audio adds 38/55 Hz engine layers, filtered brown noise, RCS hiss, resonant impact harmonics and layered detonation/noise tails. All sounds route through the existing mute/pause master; vacuum effects remain cabin sonification.

Verification: `npm test`, `npm run build`, and `git diff --check`. Browser verification covered save resume, personalization, a 175/170 kg overload trial, return to workshop with the payload intact, narrow-screen fitting and console errors. No GitHub push is part of this update.
