# Mission photo rewards

Bundled, authentic observations; no generated replacement imagery:

- titan-photo.jpg — Cassini RADAR coastal observations of Kraken Mare, PIA19046.
  Native 1600 × 900 processed radar image, not an optical photograph.
  NASA/JPL-Caltech/ASI/Cornell.
  https://science.nasa.gov/photojournal/plumbing-coastal-depths-in-titans-kraken-mare/
- moon-photo.jpg — Apollo 17 Earthrise, AS17-152-23272.
  NASA's 1920 × 1920 delivery of the astronaut photograph. NASA / Apollo 17 crew.
  https://www.nasa.gov/image-detail/amf-as17-152-23272/
- mars-photo.jpg — Jezero delta, PIA24921.
  NASA-served 4096 × 1149 enhanced-color Mastcam-Z mosaic.
  NASA/JPL-Caltech/ASU/MSSS. Image contrast/color is scientifically processed,
  rather than a claim of unprocessed natural color.
  https://science.nasa.gov/photojournal/detailed-panorama-of-mars-jezero-crater-delta/

Downloads preserve these bundled JPEGs. No official NASA or NASA Space Apps
endorsement badge is invented. The earlier Moon.jpg, Titan.jpg and Mars.jpg
remain expedition artwork and are distinct from the photograph rewards.
